Step-by-Step Guide to Get Your Session Key (Takes 15 Seconds):
  1. Go to medal.tv in your browser and log in to your account (you must be logged in for the key to exist).
  2. Press F12 on your keyboard (or right-click anywhere → Inspect) and click the Console tab at the top.
  3. Important (First-Time Browser Warning): When you try to paste in the Console, Chrome/Brave/Edge may block it and show a warning. Simply type allow pasting on your keyboard and press Enter to unlock pasting.
  4. Copy the command below, paste it into the Console, and press Enter:
JSON.parse(atob(decodeURIComponent(document.cookie.split('; ').find(r => r.startsWith('medal-auth=')).split('=')[1]))).key
5. Copy the resulting mdl_... text (without quotes), paste it into the box above, and click Download!
