Privacy Policy & Security

Personal Information & Session Tokens

This website does not store, collect, log, or sell any personally identifiable information. If you optionally provide a Medal session key to download your own private or guest-protected clips, it is transmitted strictly over encrypted HTTPS directly to the Medal.tv API for that request only. Session keys are never saved to a database, never written to disk, and never shared with any third party.

Technical Information

No user IP addresses or activity histories are stored on our servers. Video streams are proxied statelessly in real-time between Medal.tv CDNs and your browser without being saved on our servers.

Third-Party Services

To keep this service free and fast, we use Google Fonts and privacy-preserving A-Ads display banners. Single ZIP archives are streamed statelessly in real time without saving user videos on disk. These services do not use invasive tracking cookies, popunder scripts, push notification prompts, or hidden background redirects.

Open Source Verification & Inquiries

MDLDownloader is 100% open source. You can inspect the entire codebase, self-host your own instance, or report questions via our GitHub repository at github.com/yigittsahbaz/MDLDownloader.
